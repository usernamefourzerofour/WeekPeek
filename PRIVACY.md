# Privacy Policy

**WeekPeek: Calendar Widget** · Last updated: 2 November 2026

WeekPeek does not collect, store or share any personal data.

## What the app accesses

- **Your calendars.** With your permission, WeekPeek reads your calendar events through Apple's EventKit so the widget can show them. Events are read on your iPhone and never leave it.
- **Widget settings.** Your choices under *Edit Widget* (days, colors, text size and so on) are stored on your iPhone by iOS.

## What the app does not do

- No account, sign-in or registration
- No network connections: the app has no code that sends or receives data over the internet
- No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting by the developer
- No sharing or selling of data to anyone

When you tap a day, WeekPeek opens the calendar app you chose (for example Apple Calendar or Google Calendar). What that app does is covered by its own privacy policy.

## Removing access

You can turn off calendar access at any time in **Settings › Apps › WeekPeek**. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

## Children

WeekPeek is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. The full history is visible in this repository.
